# Scrum Events & Artifacts Overview

## Scrum Events Table

| **Scrum Event** | **Purpose** | **Artifacts Created / Updated** | **Who Participates** | **Exam Key Points** |
|-----------------|-------------|----------------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Sprint** *(1 month or less)* | Container for all other events; delivers usable Increment | **All artifacts evolve during the Sprint** | Scrum Team | No gap between Sprints; Sprint Goal cannot be changed once set. |
| **Sprint Planning** | Decide Sprint Goal, forecast work, plan approach | **Sprint Backlog** created (selected Product Backlog Items + plan) | Scrum Team (PO, SM, Developers) | 3 topics: Why (goal), What (scope), How (plan). |
| **Daily Scrum** *(15 min)* | Inspect progress toward Sprint Goal; adapt daily plan | **Sprint Backlog** updated (tasks, plan adjusted) | Developers | Held at same time/place daily; SM ensures it happens but Developers run it. |
| **Sprint Review** | Inspect Increment, adapt Product Backlog | **Product Backlog** updated (based on feedback), **Increment** inspected | Scrum Team + stakeholders | Not just a demo — collaborative working session. |
| **Sprint Retrospective** | Inspect & adapt team processes | No new Scrum artifact — improvement items may be added to Sprint Backlog | Scrum Team | Action items feed into next Sprint. |

---

## Artifacts Lifecycle in a Sprint

### Product Backlog
- **Owned by:** Product Owner  
- **Updated:** Continuously, especially during refinement & Sprint Review.  
- **Inspected:** Mainly in Sprint Review.  

### Sprint Backlog
- **Owned by:** Developers  
- **Created:** In Sprint Planning.  
- **Updated:** Daily Scrum + anytime work plan changes.  
- **Inspected:** Daily Scrum.  

### Increment
- **Owned by:** Developers  
- **Created:** Throughout the Sprint as PBIs meet DoD.  
- **Updated:** End of Sprint (may have multiple Increments).  
- **Inspected:** Sprint Review.  

---

## Quick Exam Mnemonic
📌 **"Plan → Do → Check → Improve"**  
- **Plan:** Sprint Planning → Sprint Backlog.  
- **Do:** Daily Scrum → Adjust Sprint Backlog.  
- **Check:** Sprint Review → Inspect Increment + Product Backlog.  
- **Improve:** Sprint Retrospective → Process changes.
